# Critique C001: Why is Jumpstart different from Bookface for YC alumni?

## The challenge

For users who are also YC alumni, Bookface already exists. It is curated, founder-only, has cohort segmentation, and has been running for years.

## How the spec answers

Two arguments, now in section 2.

1. **Bookface is alumni only.** Jumpstart is open to every accepted SS 2026 attendee from day one, before they have a YC batch.
2. **Bookface is lifelong, fragmented across batches.** Jumpstart is bounded to 90 days. The smallness is the value.

The question is whether either argument is enough on its own to justify Jumpstart's existence. Probably yes for argument 1 (most SS attendees are not in a YC batch), and partly yes for argument 2 (even alumni who use Bookface might want a sharper cohort-bounded surface for SS).

## Where the answer is weak

For YC alumni who are also SS attendees and active on Bookface, the value-add is small. They could just post in their batch group. The spec does not have a great answer for this segment.

## Mitigation

The product is for the cohort as a whole, not for the alumni subset. If 5% of SS attendees are alumni and 95% are not, the alumni overlap is fine. The product targets the 95%.

## Reopens if

YC opens Bookface to all SS attendees during the program. Then Jumpstart's positioning needs to shift from "we are not Bookface" to "we are the matchmaker layer on top of Bookface."
