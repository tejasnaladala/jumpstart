Acquisition, onboarding funnel, viral loops, launch posts, YC handoff, partnerships, post-cohort survival.

## What good looks like
- 1000 verified attendees signed up by week 4
- 30% of intro requests come from the share-card viral loop
- One YC outreach email sent at the 1000-user / 30-day mark
- Weekly cohort report shipped to investor friends (not public)
- Press placement in at least one founder publication

- This tentacle does not own moderation. Trust-safety owns that.
